The Certifications Behind Every EnvironmSafe Assessment

About Our Standards August 30, 2026

Anyone can claim to follow international safety standards. The difference between a claim and a credential is who checked, and how often. Every assessment EnvironmSafe delivers, from a fire protection audit to an LPG system evaluation, is built on certifications and standards that exist specifically so a client does not have to take our word for it.

NFPA Certified Fire Protection Specialists

Our fire protection assessments are led by NFPA Certified Fire Protection Specialists, a credential that requires demonstrated expertise across fire prevention, detection, suppression, and life safety, and that is recognized by insurers and regulators across the industries we serve. When we tell a client their suppression system has a gap, that finding carries the weight of a recognized standard behind it, not just an opinion.

API Standards Compliance

For LPG systems, process equipment, and industrial installations, our engineering and evaluation work is aligned to API standards, the reference framework insurers, regulators, and international clients expect to see cited in an assessment. Compliance to a named standard means a finding can be checked, defended, and acted on with confidence, rather than debated.

NEBOSH and IOSH Accredited Training

Training is only as credible as the body that accredits it. Our safety training programmes, delivered through both Arabic and English pathways, carry NEBOSH and IOSH accreditation, the same qualifications recognized internationally across industrial, humanitarian, and construction sectors. A NEBOSH or IOSH certificate earned through EnvironmSafe means the same thing wherever it is presented.

Why This Matters to the Clients We Serve

  • Industrial and energy clients need assessments that hold up to internal audit and external regulatory review alike.
  • NGOs operating across Yemen and the region answer to funders who require documented, standards-based evidence of safety and compliance.
  • Insurers price risk more favourably when a facility can show certified, standards-aligned assessment rather than a self-declared checklist.
  • Regulators recognize findings backed by a named international standard far more readily than an informal opinion.

Credentials Behind the Work, Not Instead of It

Standards exist to be checked, not taken on trust, which is precisely why we cite them by name in every report rather than describing our work in vaguer terms. A certification is not the deliverable. It is the reason a client can trust the deliverable. Every fire protection assessment, LPG evaluation, engineering review, and training programme we run is built on standards chosen because they are the ones our clients themselves are answerable to, whether that client is an industrial operator, an energy company, or an international NGO working across Yemen and the wider region.

When you ask who checked our work, we can name the standard, and that answer is the whole point. It is also why our assessments hold up under scrutiny long after the site visit itself is over, whether that scrutiny comes from an insurer, a funder, or an inspector.
