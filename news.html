Certified Fire Protection Can Cut Insurance Costs by Up to 85%

Fire risk is one of the costliest exposures facing industrial sites, warehouses, and NGO facilities alike — and one of the most preventable. Our NFPA Certified Fire Protection Specialists assess your facility against international fire safety standards, identify gaps before inspectors or insurers do, and help you document compliance that carriers recognize. Clients across Yemen and the region have used certified fire protection programs to cut insurance premiums by as much as 85% while meaningfully reducing risk to people and operations.
