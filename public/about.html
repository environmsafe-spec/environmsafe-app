Our Story

Who We Are

EnvironmSafe is a full-service environmental, health & safety (EHS), construction, inspection, and engineering firm with decades of combined expertise. We were founded on the belief that safety and environmental responsibility aren't just obligations — they're the foundation of every successful project.

We work with contractors, developers, municipalities, and industrial clients to navigate complex regulatory landscapes, protect their workforce, and deliver projects that meet the highest standards of quality and compliance.

From our first day of work to project closeout, we bring the expertise, responsiveness, and integrity that our clients have come to rely on.

How We Work

Our Approach

We follow a proven, structured process to deliver consistent results on every engagement.

01

Discover & Assess

02

03
