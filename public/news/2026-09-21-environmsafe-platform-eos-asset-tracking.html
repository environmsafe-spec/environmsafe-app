Our Platform Is Now Open: EOS, Asset Maintenance Tracking and Inspection

Platform Launch September 21, 2026

Most of what a service company does for you happens where you cannot see it. The visit gets scheduled somewhere. The findings get written down somewhere. The certificate gets filed somewhere. And when you need any of it six months later, you send an email and wait.

We have been steadily replacing that with systems you can log into. Today those systems have a single front door: the Platform section of this website lists every application we run, who can sign in to each one, and how to request an account.

Three systems, one place to find them

EnvironmSafe Operations Management (EOS) is the internal backbone. Jobs are raised, assigned, scheduled and closed out here, with every client record, site and asset alongside them. It is staff-only, and it exists so that nothing depends on one person remembering something.

Client Assets & Equipment Maintenance Tracking is the one most clients will care about. Every asset we service under contract is registered with its technical specification, its preventive maintenance schedule, its full fault and repair history, and the work orders and spare parts recorded against it. Our engineers see everything under contract. Each client sees their own equipment — and nobody else's.

That last point is not a detail. Access is scoped per organisation at the data layer, which means a client account cannot reach another client's records even by accident.

The Inspection & Certification platform is entering rollout now. It manages the whole inspection process end to end: scheduling the visit, the digital field checklist with photo evidence, findings and severity ratings, corrective actions, the generated report, and the certificate issued at the end with a serialised register behind it. Re-inspection dates and expiries are tracked rather than remembered.

Alongside these sit two internal tools our own team already runs on: a business agent console that handles procurement paperwork, receivables follow-up and EHS document work, and a receivables dashboard showing what is owed, how old it is and by whom.

What changes for you

Three things, mainly.

You stop asking us for documents you should already have. Reports and service histories are in the system, downloadable, at whatever hour you happen to need them.

You stop being surprised by due dates. Preventive maintenance schedules and inspection intervals are visible to you, not just to us. An overdue item is visible as overdue before it becomes a finding in somebody's audit.

And you get a straight answer about your own equipment. Not a recollection of what was probably done last time — the actual record, with dates, with who did it, with what they found.

What we are building next

The Platform page also lists what is coming, because we would rather be judged against a stated plan than a vague promise. The near-term items are a public certificate verification service, where anyone holding a certificate we issued can check it is genuine by serial number or QR code; a client portal consolidating reports, schedules, quotes and invoices; a PSV valve register built around what our certified test bench produces; and a training portal for course records and certification expiry.

Build order is set by what clients actually ask for. If one of those matters to your operation, telling us moves it up.

Getting an account

We do not run open sign-up. Every account is issued to a named person against an active contract, scoped to that organisation's data, and reviewed periodically. Staff accounts require multi-factor authentication; we strongly recommend clients enable it too. When someone leaves, tell us and we disable their access the same day.

If you hold a contract with us and want access, contact our team with the system you need and the people who need it.
