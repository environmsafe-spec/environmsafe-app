Why Calibration Matters

A Measurement Is Only as Reliable as the Instrument Behind It

Instruments drift. A balance, pressure gauge, gas detector or patient monitor that is out of calibration can pass a bad batch, reject a good one, miss a hazard or put a safety limit in the wrong place. Usually nobody notices until an audit or an incident.

EnvironmSafe provides calibration through external partner laboratories accredited to ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Our principal partner is accredited by the Saudi Accreditation Center (SAAC) and the International Accreditation Service (IAS). A second partner laboratory, accredited by EA-JAS, adds biomedical, volumetric, analytical and gas-detection equipment. We are your single point of contact. We review your equipment, plan the schedule, coordinate the work and deliver the certificates. Each calibration is performed by the accredited laboratory, within its scope.

FAQ

Calibration — Common Questions

Who performs the calibration?

External partner laboratories accredited to ISO/IEC 17025:2017. Our principal partner is accredited by the Saudi Accreditation Center (SAAC) and the International Accreditation Service (IAS). A second partner laboratory, accredited by EA-JAS, covers additional biomedical, volumetric, analytical and gas-detection equipment. EnvironmSafe manages the service from start to finish and remains your point of contact.

Is every calibration point accredited?

Most of the range above is within the laboratories' accredited scope. Where an instrument or measurement point is not, we tell you before we quote, and the certificate marks it clearly as not accredited. You always know exactly what you are getting.

What does the calibration certificate include?

The instrument's identification, the reference standards used and their traceability, the measured results, the measurement uncertainty (k = 2, about 95% confidence), the calibration and due dates, and the environmental conditions at calibration. On request, it also states whether the instrument conforms to the manufacturer's specification.

Is calibration done on site or in the laboratory?

In the laboratory. Instruments are sent to the accredited partner laboratory and returned with their certificates, and we coordinate the transport. On-site calibration of large and installed equipment is coming soon; see the list above to register your interest.

How often should instruments be calibrated?

The default interval on the certificate and label is one year, unless you specify a different one. The right interval depends on the manufacturer's recommendation, how heavily and where the instrument is used, how critical its readings are, and what your quality system requires. We can help you set it for each instrument.

What happens if an instrument fails calibration?

If an instrument is found faulty or well outside tolerance, the laboratory notifies us and we contact you before any further work. Calibration does not include repair; we will advise you on the options.

Is this the same as PSV testing?

No. Pressure safety valves are set-pressure tested and certified on our own test bench as a separate service. See PSV Testing & Calibration. This service covers measuring and test instruments: the gauges, meters, sensors and analysers your readings come from.

Instruments Due for Calibration?

Send us your equipment list with the make, model and range of each instrument. We will confirm the scope, schedule and price.
