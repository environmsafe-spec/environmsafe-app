Why Calibration Matters

A Measurement Is Only as Reliable as the Instrument Behind It

Instruments drift. A balance, gauge or thermometer that is out of calibration can pass a bad batch, reject a good one, or put a safety limit in the wrong place. Usually nobody notices until an audit or an incident.

EnvironmSafe provides calibration for laboratory and instrumentation equipment through an external partner laboratory accredited by the Saudi Accreditation Center (SAAC) and the International Accreditation Service (IAS). We are your single point of contact. We review your equipment, plan the schedule, coordinate the work and deliver the certificates. The accredited laboratory performs each calibration within its accredited scope.

FAQ

Calibration — Common Questions

Who performs the calibration?

An external partner laboratory accredited by the Saudi Accreditation Center (SAAC) and the International Accreditation Service (IAS) performs each calibration within its accredited scope. EnvironmSafe manages the service from start to finish and remains your point of contact.

What does the calibration certificate include?

The instrument's identification, the results as found and, where adjusted, as left, the measurement uncertainty, the reference standards used and their traceability, and the calibration date. These are the details an auditor checks.

Is calibration done on site or in the laboratory?

It depends on the instrument. Some can be calibrated at your facility; others must go to the laboratory. We confirm this for every item when we quote, and plan the work to keep your downtime to a minimum.

How often should instruments be calibrated?

It depends on the manufacturer's recommendation, how heavily and where the instrument is used, how critical its readings are, and what your quality system requires. Annual calibration is common. We can help you set a sensible interval for each instrument.

Is this the same as PSV testing?

No. Pressure safety valves are set-pressure tested and certified on our own test bench as a separate service. See PSV Testing & Calibration. This service covers measuring instruments: the gauges, meters and sensors your readings come from.

Instruments Due for Calibration?

Send us your equipment list with make, model and range. We will confirm scope, schedule and price.
