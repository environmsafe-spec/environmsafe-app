Getting In

How Access Works

We do not run open sign-up. Every account is issued to a named person against a contract or an employment record.

1

Request an account

Contact us with the system you need, your organisation and the people who need access. Client accounts are tied to an active contract.

2

We verify and scope it

We confirm the request with your authorised contact, then decide what the account should see — your sites and assets only, nothing else.

3

Credentials are issued

You receive an invitation e-mail and set your own password from the invitation link. The account is scoped to your organisation on the way in — there is nothing to configure at your end.

4

Access is reviewed

Accounts are reviewed periodically and revoked when someone leaves or a contract ends. Tell us about a leaver and we will disable access the same day.

How we protect these systems

These applications hold inspection findings, certificates and asset data that our clients depend on being accurate and private. They are built and operated accordingly.

Encrypted in transit

Every application is served over HTTPS with certificates renewed automatically. No system accepts unencrypted connections.

Role-based access

What you can see and change is set by your role. Client accounts are scoped to their own organisation's data and cannot reach anyone else's.

Invitation-only accounts

There is no open sign-up anywhere on the platform. Every account is created by invitation to a named person, who sets their own password from the invitation link.

Audit trail

Inspection records and certificates carry a full history — who recorded what, when it was changed, and by whom. Issued certificates are never silently edited.

Backed up

Data is backed up on a regular schedule with restores tested, so a certificate issued years ago is still retrievable.

Isolated systems

Each application runs on its own subdomain and its own environment. A problem in one does not put the others at risk.
