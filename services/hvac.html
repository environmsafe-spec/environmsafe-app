HVAC Services

Heating, ventilation, and air conditioning solutions for commercial and industrial facilities — designed for efficiency, comfort, and air quality.

Our HVAC Expertise

Optimal Climate. Superior Air Quality.

Proper HVAC systems are essential not just for comfort — they directly impact air quality, energy efficiency, occupational health, and regulatory compliance. EnvironmSafe designs, installs, and maintains HVAC systems for commercial and industrial facilities with a focus on performance and long-term reliability.

Our HVAC specialists bring deep expertise in industrial ventilation, clean room systems, exhaust and air handling, and energy-efficient climate control — ensuring your facility meets ASHRAE standards and local building codes.

What We Offer

HVAC Service Areas

Complete HVAC solutions from design through long-term maintenance for commercial and industrial applications.

HVAC System Design

Custom HVAC system design tailored to the specific load requirements, layout, and use of your facility.

  • Load calculations & system sizing
  • Ductwork design & layout
  • ASHRAE standards compliance
  • Energy modeling & optimization

Industrial Ventilation

Specialized industrial ventilation systems to control airborne contaminants, hazardous fumes, and process exhaust.

  • Local exhaust ventilation (LEV) design
  • General dilution ventilation
  • Fume hood & capture systems
  • OSHA ventilation compliance

Air Quality & Filtration

Advanced air filtration and purification solutions to improve indoor air quality and meet health and safety standards.

  • HEPA & MERV-rated filtration
  • UV-C air purification systems
  • Humidity & pressure control
  • Clean room HVAC design

Installation & Commissioning

Professional HVAC installation with full system commissioning to ensure optimal performance from day one.

  • Equipment procurement & installation
  • System balancing & testing
  • Controls & BMS integration
  • Commissioning reports

Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled preventive maintenance programs to maximize equipment lifespan, efficiency, and reliability.

  • Seasonal system tune-ups
  • Filter replacement programs
  • Coil cleaning & inspection
  • Performance monitoring

Energy-Efficiency Upgrades

Retrofit and upgrade services to modernize aging HVAC systems and reduce energy consumption and operating costs.

  • Energy audits & benchmarking
  • High-efficiency equipment upgrades
  • Variable speed drive installation
  • Smart controls & automation
