Our Programs

Training Programs We Offer

Targeted programs designed to address your organization's most critical safety training needs.

Safe Operations for Lifting Equipment

A comprehensive training program covering the safe use, inspection, and operation of lifting equipment. Participants gain practical knowledge of load calculations, rigging techniques, equipment inspection protocols, and regulatory compliance to prevent incidents and ensure safe lifting operations on-site.

  • Load calculations & weight distribution
  • Rigging techniques & sling selection
  • Pre-use equipment inspection protocols
  • Hazard identification during lifting operations
  • Regulatory compliance & lift planning
  • Practical on-site exercises

HSE Transformation — From Zero to International Standards

A structured program that guides organizations through a complete HSE transformation journey — from establishing foundational safety practices to achieving full alignment with international standards such as ISO 45001, ISO 14001, and OSHA benchmarks. We work closely with your team to assess current gaps, build robust HSE management systems, and instill a lasting culture of safety excellence.

  • Current-state HSE gap assessment
  • ISO 45001 & ISO 14001 alignment roadmap
  • HSE policy & procedure development
  • Safety culture awareness & leadership engagement
  • Risk management system implementation
  • OSHA benchmark compliance planning

IOSH Working Safely

An internationally recognized, one-day introductory course accredited by IOSH (Institution of Occupational Safety and Health). Designed for workers at all levels across any industry, it provides a clear understanding of health & safety responsibilities and how every individual contributes to a safer workplace. Participants receive an IOSH certificate upon successful completion.

  • Understanding health & safety responsibilities
  • Identifying and assessing workplace hazards
  • Accident causes, costs & prevention
  • Personal protective measures & safe behaviours
  • Emergency procedures & incident reporting
  • Internationally recognized IOSH certificate

IOSH Managing Safely

A globally respected, multi-day program accredited by IOSH, designed specifically for managers and supervisors in any sector. It equips leaders with the practical knowledge to manage H&S within their teams — covering risk assessment, incident investigation, legal responsibilities, and building a proactive safety culture. Participants are assessed and awarded the internationally recognized IOSH Managing Safely certificate.

  • Assessing and controlling risks in the workplace
  • Investigating incidents & near misses
  • Understanding managers' legal H&S responsibilities
  • Measuring & monitoring safety performance
  • Building a proactive health & safety culture
  • Internationally recognized IOSH certificate
NEBOSH Accredited

NEBOSH-Certified Programs

NEBOSH (National Examination Board in Occupational Safety and Health) qualifications are the world's most widely recognized health, safety, and environmental credentials — trusted by employers in over 130 countries and spanning every industry. EnvironmSafe delivers the full suite of NEBOSH programs, from accessible entry-level awards to the prestigious Level 6 International Diploma.

NEBOSH Health and Safety at Work Award

An accessible entry-level NEBOSH qualification that gives every worker a solid grounding in workplace health and safety — no prior experience required. Ideal for inductions and baseline safety competence across any industry.

  • Core health & safety responsibilities
  • Workplace hazard identification
  • Personal protective measures
  • Basic risk concepts & controls
  • NEBOSH Award certificate upon completion

NEBOSH Environmental Awareness at Work Award

Designed to raise environmental literacy across the workforce — covering key environmental issues, legislation, and each worker's individual responsibility in protecting the environment. A powerful foundation for any organization's sustainability agenda.

  • Environmental legislation fundamentals
  • Waste management & pollution control
  • Energy & resource conservation
  • Environmental incident prevention
  • NEBOSH Environmental Award certificate

NEBOSH HSE Introduction to Incident Investigation

Developed in direct partnership with the UK's Health and Safety Executive (HSE), this course teaches a structured, evidence-based approach to investigating workplace incidents — turning investigations into genuine learning opportunities that prevent recurrence.

  • Why incidents happen — causation models
  • Immediate, underlying & root cause analysis
  • Evidence gathering & witness interviewing
  • Structured investigation methodology
  • NEBOSH/HSE certificate awarded

NEBOSH International General Certificate (IGC) — Arabic

The globally respected NEBOSH IGC — the world's most popular health and safety qualification — delivered entirely in Arabic. Making world-class safety credentials fully accessible to Arabic-speaking professionals across the region, with no language barrier standing between talent and achievement.

  • Managing health & safety (Unit NG1)
  • Risk assessment & control strategies
  • Workplace, fire, electrical & chemical hazards
  • Environmental & occupational health
  • Practical assessment (Unit NG2)
  • NEBOSH IGC certificate — Arabic pathway

NEBOSH International General Certificate (IGC) — English

The benchmark NEBOSH IGC in Health & Safety — the single most recognized H&S qualification in the world, held by over 200,000 professionals globally. It equips candidates with the knowledge and confidence to manage workplace health and safety to an internationally recognized standard.

  • Managing health & safety (Unit NG1)
  • Risk assessment & control strategies
  • Workplace, fire, electrical & chemical hazards
  • Environmental & occupational health
  • Practical assessment (Unit NG2)
  • NEBOSH IGC certificate — English pathway

NEBOSH Process Safety Management (PSM)

A specialist NEBOSH qualification for professionals in high-hazard industries — oil & gas, chemical, petrochemical, and process manufacturing. Covers the technical and managerial disciplines needed to prevent catastrophic major accident hazards and build industry-leading process safety cultures.

  • Process hazard identification & HAZOP
  • Major accident hazard (MAH) controls
  • Bow-tie & barrier analysis
  • Safety instrumented systems (SIS)
  • Process safety leadership & culture
  • NEBOSH PSM certificate awarded

NEBOSH Level 6 International Diploma

The pinnacle of NEBOSH achievement — a degree-equivalent (RQF Level 6) professional credential that positions graduates for senior and strategic health & safety roles. Globally recognized by employers and professional bodies including IOSH, the NEBOSH International Diploma represents the highest tier of H&S expertise and opens the pathway to Chartered membership (CMIOSH).

  • Advanced risk management & principles
  • Strategic H&S management systems
  • Workplace & work equipment (advanced)
  • Environmental management (advanced)
  • Pathway to CMIOSH chartered membership
  • NEBOSH Level 6 International Diploma awarded
