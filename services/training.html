Our Programs

Training Programs We Offer

Targeted programs designed to address your organization's most critical safety training needs.

Safe Operations for Lifting Equipment

A comprehensive training program covering the safe use, inspection, and operation of lifting equipment. Participants gain practical knowledge of load calculations, rigging techniques, equipment inspection protocols, and regulatory compliance to prevent incidents and ensure safe lifting operations on-site.

  • Load calculations & weight distribution
  • Rigging techniques & sling selection
  • Pre-use equipment inspection protocols
  • Hazard identification during lifting operations
  • Regulatory compliance & lift planning
  • Practical on-site exercises

HSE Transformation — From Zero to International Standards

A structured program that guides organizations through a complete HSE transformation journey — from establishing foundational safety practices to achieving full alignment with international standards such as ISO 45001, ISO 14001, and OSHA benchmarks. We work closely with your team to assess current gaps, build robust HSE management systems, and instill a lasting culture of safety excellence.

  • Current-state HSE gap assessment
  • ISO 45001 & ISO 14001 alignment roadmap
  • HSE policy & procedure development
  • Safety culture awareness & leadership engagement
  • Risk management system implementation
  • OSHA benchmark compliance planning
