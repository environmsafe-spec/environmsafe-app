Why Inspection & Monitoring Matters

See Problems Before They Become Crises

Routine inspections and continuous environmental monitoring are the first line of defense against compliance failures, structural deficiencies, and environmental incidents. EnvironmSafe provides independent, technically sound inspection and monitoring services that give you real-time visibility into your project's status.

Our inspectors hold industry certifications across structural, environmental, and safety disciplines, ensuring every assessment meets the highest standards of accuracy and integrity.

What We Offer

Inspection & Monitoring Services

Comprehensive inspection and monitoring services across structural, environmental, and compliance domains.

Structural Inspections

Thorough structural assessments of buildings, bridges, retaining walls, and industrial facilities to ensure safety and code compliance.

  • Building condition assessments
  • Foundation & structural integrity checks
  • Pre-construction & final inspections
  • Code compliance verification

Air Quality Monitoring

Ambient and indoor air quality monitoring to identify contaminants, track emissions, and ensure regulatory compliance.

  • Ambient air quality sampling
  • Stack emission testing
  • Indoor air quality investigations
  • VOC & particulate monitoring

Water Quality Monitoring

Surface water and groundwater monitoring programs designed to detect contamination and demonstrate regulatory compliance.

  • Groundwater sampling & analysis
  • Surface water monitoring
  • NPDES compliance monitoring
  • Stormwater runoff assessments

Continuous Monitoring Systems

Design and deployment of automated, real-time monitoring systems for environmental and safety parameters.

  • Remote sensor networks
  • Real-time data dashboards
  • Alert & notification systems
  • Data management & reporting

Equipment & Facility Audits

Systematic inspection of industrial equipment and facilities to identify maintenance needs, safety hazards, and compliance gaps.

  • Equipment condition assessments
  • Safety system testing & verification
  • Facility walkthrough audits
  • Maintenance recommendations

Compliance Inspections

Regulatory compliance inspections against EPA, OSHA, local, and industry-specific standards, with detailed gap-analysis reports.

  • Third-party compliance reviews
  • Regulatory agency support
  • Corrective action tracking
  • Detailed inspection reports

LEEA Certified Lifting Equipment Inspection

Our LEEA (Lifting Equipment Engineers Association) certified team provides thorough inspection and certification of all types of lifting equipment to ensure safe operation and regulatory compliance.

  • Crane inspection & load testing
  • Forklift & telehandler certification
  • Rigging & lifting accessories inspection
  • Certified lifting equipment reports

NFPA & API Certified Pressurized Systems Inspection

Certified inspection of pressurized systems including boiler packages, pressurized air packages, pressure safety valves, and pressure vessels — by our NFPA and API 510 certified team.

  • Boiler package inspection & certification
  • Pressurized air system assessment
  • Pressure Safety Valve (PSV) testing
  • Pressure vessel inspection & certification (API 510)

Certified NDT Inspection & Reporting

Our certified QC inspectors perform Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) to detect structural flaws without damaging the asset, delivering detailed certified reports.

  • Ultrasonic testing (UT)
  • Magnetic particle & dye penetrant testing
  • Visual & dimensional inspection
  • Detailed NDT reports & certification
