What We Offer

Safety Evaluation Service Areas

Comprehensive safety evaluations across critical industrial systems and infrastructure.

LPG Systems Safety Evaluation

End-to-end safety assessment of LPG storage, distribution, and utilization systems to identify hazards and ensure regulatory compliance.

  • LPG storage tank inspection & integrity assessment
  • Pipe network leak detection & pressure testing
  • Safety valve and pressure relief verification
  • NFPA 58 / local regulatory compliance review

Boiler & Steam System Assessment

Thorough evaluation of boiler systems, steam lines, and associated controls to prevent failures and ensure safe, efficient operation.

  • Boiler pressure vessel inspection
  • Safety control & interlock verification
  • Steam trap & distribution line assessment
  • ASME & local jurisdiction compliance

Electrical Safety Evaluation

Systematic assessment of electrical installations, distribution panels, and earthing systems to prevent hazards and ensure code compliance.

  • Electrical panel & switchgear inspection
  • Earthing & bonding system verification
  • Arc flash hazard analysis
  • IEC / NEC code compliance review

Pressurized Vessels & Systems

Inspection and evaluation of pressure vessels, piping systems, and containment equipment per international standards.

  • Pressure vessel fitness-for-service evaluation
  • Thickness measurement & corrosion assessment
  • Relief device testing & certification
  • API 510 & API 570 inspection standards

Fire Protection System Evaluation

Assessment of fire detection, suppression, and alarm systems to verify readiness and standards compliance.

  • Sprinkler & suppression system review
  • Fire alarm panel & detector inspection
  • Emergency response system readiness
  • NFPA 13, 72 & 101 compliance verification

Safety Risk Assessment & Reporting

Integrated risk assessments that quantify hazard severity and provide actionable recommendations to prioritize corrective actions.

  • Hazard identification & risk ranking
  • Regulatory gap analysis
  • Corrective action plan development
  • Insurance & compliance documentation
