Informed Decisions Start with Accurate Evaluations

Every successful project begins with a clear understanding of site conditions, regulatory requirements, and potential risks. EnvironmSafe's evaluation services provide the objective, technically rigorous assessments you need to move forward with confidence.

What We Offer

Evaluation Service Areas

Comprehensive evaluation services across environmental, safety, and operational domains.

Environmental Site Assessments

ASTM-standard Phase I and Phase II ESAs to identify recognized environmental conditions and subsurface contamination.

  • Phase I ESA — records review & site reconnaissance
  • Phase II ESA — soil & groundwater sampling
  • Regulatory database searches
  • ASTM E1527-21 compliance

Risk Evaluation & Assessment

Quantitative and qualitative risk evaluations to understand exposure pathways and prioritize remediation or controls.

  • Human health risk assessments
  • Ecological risk assessments
  • Exposure pathway analysis
  • Risk-based corrective action

Compliance Evaluations

Independent regulatory compliance evaluations against federal, state, and local environmental and safety requirements.

  • RCRA, CERCLA, Clean Water Act reviews
  • Air permit compliance assessments
  • Stormwater & NPDES evaluations
  • Corrective action plans

Due Diligence & Property Evaluations

Transaction-support evaluations for property acquisitions, mergers, and real estate deals involving environmental liability.

  • Environmental liability screening
  • Lender & investor-grade reports
  • Portfolio property assessments
  • Cost-to-remediate estimates

Feasibility Studies

Technical and economic feasibility analyses to identify the most practical and cost-effective path forward.

  • Remediation technology screening
  • Comparative cost analysis
  • Alternative evaluation matrices
  • Regulatory acceptance probability

Performance & Baseline Evaluations

Establish environmental and safety baselines and track performance metrics over time to drive continuous improvement.

  • Environmental baseline studies
  • KPI development & tracking
  • Third-party performance reviews
  • Benchmarking against best practices
